# Known Limitations

This file records current evidence and implementation boundaries. A limitation is not a semantic judgment.

## Current boundaries

- R3 changes are not established until their exact execution and post-landing verification complete.
- The forty-two historical repositories remain live until a separately authorized archive stage.
- The successor mechanical reproduction profile is deliberately narrow; it does not evaluate semantic correctness, evidentiary weight, safety, compliance, readiness, or downstream system behavior.
- Relationship predicate vocabularies and mappings remain profile-/authority-scoped rather than universal Core semantics.
- The current Core extension rule states opaque preservation/no structural dereference; interoperability behavior still requires implementation fixtures and qualification before F4 closure.
- Security and supply-chain controls are bounded technical properties and require their own evidence; they are not implied by a mechanical PASS.
- External legal review remains required if non-MIT or third-party material later enters Core.
- Historical evidence remains historical evidence. It is not silently transferred to the successor topology.

A limitation may be reduced only by new evidence or an explicit project decision within the relevant scope.

# Use Case Profiles

**Status**: EXAMPLES / NOT DEPLOYMENT CLAIMS

These profiles are examples of where Riverbraid patterns may be useful. They are not claims of current adoption.

## AI governance lab

Uses Riverbraid to separate model or workflow claims from evidence, verification depth, and known limitations.

## Government transparency office

Uses Riverbraid-style claim records to make public-process evidence easier to inspect.

## Creative AI systems studio

Uses Riverbraid to preserve provenance, consent boundaries, and artifact history without flattening creative context.

## Data-center or AI infrastructure pilot

Uses Riverbraid-style evidence records for energy, water, heat reuse, grid flexibility, and public benefit claims.

## Research reproducibility group

Uses Riverbraid to map claims to commits, workflows, expected outputs, and known limits.

## Boundary

These are hypothetical profiles. They do not claim deployment, endorsement, certification, production readiness, or external review.

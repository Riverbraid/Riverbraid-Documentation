# Riverbraid in 10 Minutes

## What Riverbraid is

Riverbraid is an open evidence fabric for making bounded claims and their support inspectable without making Riverbraid the authority over meaning.

Its minimal target is to preserve:

- what is claimed;
- who or what declared it;
- what evidence is referenced;
- what relationships are asserted and by whom;
- what scope and conditions apply;
- what remains unavailable, uncertain, challenged, or unresolved;
- where multiple interpretations legitimately diverge.

## What Riverbraid does not do

Riverbraid does not decide which interpretation is correct. It does not assign universal trust, evidentiary weight, legitimacy, moral priority, risk, readiness, certification, or general suitability.

A relationship recorded by Riverbraid is an attributed assertion. Recording it does not make the relationship true by Riverbraid authority.

## Current architecture

The current public architecture has five active architectural repositories, five explicitly experimental/noncontrolling repositories, and forty-two historical repositories pending later archive.

`Riverbraid-Core` controls only the mechanical interoperability grammar. `Riverbraid-Evaluation-Kit` owns a declared mechanical reproduction profile. `Riverbraid-Governance` records Riverbraid project decisions. `Riverbraid-Documentation` controls currentness navigation only. `.github` is routing/community infrastructure.

## Where to start

Read `CURRENT.md`, then `ARCHITECTURE_MAP.md`, `SYSTEM_MAP.md`, and `WHAT_PASS_MEANS.md`.

The point is not to accept Riverbraid's conclusion. The point is to be able to inspect what a bounded result actually establishes and what it leaves open.

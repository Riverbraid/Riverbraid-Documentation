# Riverbraid in 10 Minutes

**Status**: FIRST-READER GUIDE  
**Claim boundary**: proof-of-concept orientation only

## What Riverbraid is

Riverbraid is an open-source deterministic integrity floor for AI governance.

It is designed to make structure, authority, evidence, drift, and failure visible before trust is granted.

## The problem

AI systems can produce plausible outputs without stable evidence, provenance, or claim boundaries. That creates avoidable rework, trust inflation, and governance confusion.

## The Riverbraid response

Riverbraid separates:

- what is claimed
- what is evidenced
- what is verified
- what failed
- what remains unknown

It uses fail-closed verification, pinned evidence surfaces, and explicit non-claims.

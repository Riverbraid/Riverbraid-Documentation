# Forks and Adaptations

**Status**: GOVERNANCE GUIDANCE  
**Claim boundary**: fork/adaptation policy draft

## Permission to adapt

Riverbraid is open source. Projects may fork, modify, and adapt it according to the applicable repository license.

## Required adaptation boundary

Any adaptation should clearly state its divergence from canonical Riverbraid surfaces.

Recommended root file:

```text
ADAPTATION.md
```

That file should explain:

- what changed
- what evidence exists
- what claims are made
- what claims are not made
- what remains unverified
- whether the adaptation changes protocol, registry, workflow, verifier, evidence, or claim behavior

## Labels to avoid without a future governance process

Avoid:

- Riverbraid-certified
- officially certified
- production approved
- externally audited
- canonical alignment
- official endorsement

unless a future Riverbraid governance process explicitly grants that status.

## Safer labels

Use:

- Riverbraid-aligned pilot
- Riverbraid-inspired implementation
- Riverbraid-compatible draft
- Riverbraid-based governance surface

## Boundary

This file is a claim-boundary and adaptation guidance surface. It does not create certification, external audit, or production readiness.

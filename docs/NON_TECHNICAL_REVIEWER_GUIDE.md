# Plain Language Reviewer Guide

**Status**: Orientation

## Purpose

This guide helps reviewers inspect Riverbraid without command-line work.

## What to review

- Is the repository role clear?
- Are claims and non-claims visible?
- Is evidence linked or described?
- Are limitations easy to find?
- Is PASS explained without overclaiming?

## Main surfaces

- `Riverbraid-Evaluation-Kit`: public evaluation path.
- `Riverbraid-Core`: protocol authority surface.
- `Riverbraid-Documentation`: explanation and roadmap.
- Gold repositories: bounded domain surfaces.

## PASS and FAIL

PASS means the stated check passed for its stated scope.

FAIL means stronger claims should stop until the issue is understood.

## Filing a concern

Open an issue and include repository, file, observed wording, evidence, and suggested correction.

## Boundary

This guide is explanatory. It does not create stronger claims.

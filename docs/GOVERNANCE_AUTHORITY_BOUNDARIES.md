# Governance Authority Boundaries

Riverbraid separates authority types so that project control does not become authority over meaning.

## Mechanical protocol authority

`Riverbraid-Core` controls Riverbraid's mechanical interoperability grammar only.

It may define structural requirements needed for interoperable records and attributed assertions. It does not decide truth, trust, evidentiary weight, legitimacy, moral priority, risk, readiness, certification, or correct interpretation.

## Declared profile/vocabulary authority

A named profile may define a bounded evaluation contract. A named vocabulary may define namespaced predicate meanings.

Their authority is attributable and scoped. Neither becomes universal Riverbraid meaning merely because Riverbraid can reference it.

## Riverbraid project authority

`Riverbraid-Governance` records Riverbraid's own adoption, supersession, release, archive, stewardship, and decision process.

Project authority may decide which Riverbraid artifact is current for this project. It cannot make an external claim true or settle a legitimate interpretive dispute by project fiat.

## Documentation authority

`Riverbraid-Documentation` controls currentness navigation within its declared scope only.

## Build-control authority

F0-F4 qualification, hashes, custody records, execution cards, stop conditions, and internal acceptance gates govern the Riverbraid build process. They do not automatically become public protocol requirements.

## Disagreement

Riverbraid should preserve attributable disagreement, challenge, uncertainty, and alternative interpretation rather than requiring a single semantic authority to resolve them.

# FAQ and Misconceptions

**Status**: PUBLIC COHERENCE / NON-CLAIM GUIDE

## Does Riverbraid measure human worth?

No. Riverbraid evaluates claims, artifacts, processes, evidence, and verification surfaces. It does not assign universal human worth.

## Is Riverbraid a social credit system?

No. Riverbraid is not a human scoring system. It is a claim-boundary and evidence-integrity architecture.

## Does fail-closed mean punishment?

No. Fail-closed means that when evidence is missing or a check fails, stronger claims stop until the issue is resolved.

## Can Riverbraid be used to make decisions about people?

Only with extreme care and only if the decision process itself is bounded, transparent, evidence-based, rights-respecting, and governed by appropriate human, legal, and ethical review. Riverbraid does not grant authority to make decisions about people.

## Is Riverbraid finished?

Phase 4 proof-of-concept scope is closed. Security hardening, registry succession, verification-depth upgrades, external review, and production-readiness work remain open.

## Does PASS mean secure?

No. PASS means the configured verifier passed for the stated scope. It does not mean secure, certified, externally audited, production ready, or defect free.

## Is Riverbraid certified?

No. Riverbraid currently makes no certification claim.

## Can I fork Riverbraid?

Yes. Forks and adaptations should clearly state what changed, what evidence exists, and what claims are not made.

## What does canonical mean?

Canonical means the surface currently serves as a reference authority for a bounded Riverbraid role. It does not imply production readiness or external audit.

## What is an adaptation versus official Riverbraid?

An adaptation is a fork or applied version. It must not claim official certification, external audit, or canonical authority unless a future governance process grants that status.

## Boundary

This FAQ is explanatory. It does not change any protocol, registry, verifier, workflow, release, tag, hash, seal, manifest, or secret.

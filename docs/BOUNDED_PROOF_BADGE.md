# Bounded Proof Badge

**Status**: VISUAL LANGUAGE / CLAIM BOUNDARY

## Purpose

A bounded proof badge is a compact marker linking to a specific evidence boundary.

It should say what was checked, what was not checked, and where the evidence lives.

## Recommended wording

```text
Riverbraid Bounded Proof Badge
Phase 4 Proof-of-Concept Scope
Verified Evidence Surface
```

## Means

The stated proof-of-concept scope passed its documented evidence checks.

## Does not mean

- certification
- production readiness
- legal approval
- complete safety
- absence of defects
- official approval of forks or adaptations

## Boundary

This badge is not certification language.

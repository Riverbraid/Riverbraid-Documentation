## Purpose

This ledger maps Riverbraid claims to evidence, limitations, and non-claims.

## Format

| Claim | Evidence | Limitation | Non-claims | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 4 proof-of-concept scope is closed | Evidence files 90, 91, 93, 94 | Bounded to proof-of-concept scope | No certification, production readiness, or security-hardening claim | PLACEHOLDER_PENDING_EXACT_PATHS |

## Rule

```text
Claims must remain smaller than or equal to available evidence.
```

## Boundary

This ledger does not create new claims. It records claim-to-evidence boundaries.

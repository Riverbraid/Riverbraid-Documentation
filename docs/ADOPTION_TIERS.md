# Adoption Tiers

**Status**: Orientation

Riverbraid adoption can happen at different depths.

| Tier | Role |
|---|---|
| Reader | Reads the documentation and boundaries. |
| Inspector | Runs or reviews the Evaluation Kit. |
| Adapter | Forks or adapts a surface for a local context. |
| Implementer | Builds a domain-specific surface. |
| Reviewer | Reviews evidence, registry state, and limitations. |

## Rule

No tier inherits a stronger claim than the evidence supports.

## Boundary

These tiers are descriptive. They do not create approval, readiness, or review claims.
